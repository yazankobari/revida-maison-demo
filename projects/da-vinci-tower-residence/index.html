High above the Dubai Water Canal, the living room gathers around a long fire wall in grey stone, with deep sofas, orange leather and a low glass table.

Black marble, smoked wood and herringbone floors carry through the kitchen, the bathrooms and four bedrooms, each with its own accent: sage, ember and burnt orange.
