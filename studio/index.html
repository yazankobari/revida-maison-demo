A clear brief, before a first line.

We begin with your property and the way you want to use it. Together, we identify what works, what needs to change and where to focus.

What you leave with

A shared project brief

Your priorities, budget range, timing and the rooms or areas to include.

Your part

Agree the priorities before developing the design.

What can I bring to the first conversation?

Photos of the space, any plans you have, and a few references you love. Your priorities, timing and a comfortable budget help shape the brief.

Your routines. Your priorities. Your starting point.

See the choices. Feel the difference.

Layouts show how the space will work. Visual references and physical samples help you compare the atmosphere, finishes and details before choosing a direction.

What you leave with

An agreed design direction

A layout and a coordinated palette, with drawings and specifications appropriate to the agreed work.

Your part

Review the layout and finishes before moving ahead.

How do we make the decisions together?

Layouts and visual references give the conversation shape. Material samples, finish options and practical details help you understand the choices before they are agreed.

A clear layout. A material connection. A careful junction.

The finish deserves the same care.

For projects that include implementation, coordination and finishing checks carry the agreed design into the space. Small details are reviewed before handover.

What you leave with

A considered handover

Review of the agreed work and a record of any outstanding finishing items.

Your part

Walk through the space and review the final details together.

What happens before handover?

The agreed work is reviewed, finishing details are checked and any outstanding items are recorded. The handover brings the decisions back to the way you will use the space.

Details checked. Decisions connected. A space ready for life.

Where do we begin?

With your space, your priorities and your budget. Bring the photos, plans or ideas you already have; we will explore the possibilities together.

Can you work on both homes and commercial spaces?

Yes. Revida Maison works with apartments, villas and commercial interiors, shaping each space around the people who use it.

Do you take a project from design to completion?

Revida Maison brings interior design, renovation, finishing, custom joinery and furnishing together, from the first conversation to handover.
