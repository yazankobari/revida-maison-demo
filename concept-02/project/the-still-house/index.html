01 / The intention

A quieter
way to
come home.

Make less room for things.
More room for living.

The starting point was a familiar room with too many competing voices. The intention was to listen for what mattered: daylight, honest materials, a little room to breathe.

A flat plane overhead. A rhythm of smoked oak. Three slender openings onto the courtyard. Everything else finds its place around the life of the room.
