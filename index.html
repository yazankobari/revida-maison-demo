A Few Good
Questions.

Clear answers before the first conversation. Your space, your priorities and the possibilities ahead.

Where do we begin?+

With your space, your priorities and your budget. Bring the photos, plans or ideas you already have; we will explore the possibilities together.

Can you work on both homes and commercial spaces?+

Yes. Revida Maison works with apartments, villas and commercial interiors, shaping each space around the people who use it.

Do you take a project from design to completion?+

Revida Maison brings interior design, renovation, finishing, custom joinery and furnishing together, from the first conversation to handover.

Can I see how a space changes?+

Explore the project studies above. Each connects the existing space with its design decisions, materials and imagined final result.

What should I bring to the first conversation?+

A few photos, the location and any plans you have. A rough budget and your priorities help us understand where to begin.
