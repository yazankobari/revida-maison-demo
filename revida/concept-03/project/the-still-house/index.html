Residential / An interior conversation ⟶

A little less noise.
A little more feeling.

The Still House is an imagined residence shaped by the soft weight of linen, the irregularity of stone and the changing light of an ordinary afternoon.

Its three courtyard windows set the rhythm. The materials do the rest.

How it feels.
